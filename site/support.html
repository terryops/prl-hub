Quick help

Back up your wallet. Your 12-word recovery phrase is the only way to restore your wallet. Write it down offline and never share it — anyone with it can move your funds. It is stored encrypted on your device and is never uploaded.

Add a mining monitor. Go to My Monitor → Add, pick your pool (AlphaPool, Lucky Pool, TW-Pool, HeroMiners or Pearl Fortune) and paste your Pearl receiving address to track hashrate, workers and on-chain income.

Estimate profit. In Mining Monitor, compare GPUs and set electricity price, pool fee and rent to see daily / 30 / 90-day net profit.

Sync across devices. One purchase covers iPhone, iPad and Mac, and your settings sync privately via iCloud.
